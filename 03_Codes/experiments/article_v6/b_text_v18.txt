A wildfire DSS draws on heterogeneous sources, none of which observes the fire state directly. VODA therefore reads ten bounded features computed from observations, each observable, bounded, meaningful to an expert, and not redundant with the others. They are fire intensity {m:z_1} and spread potential {m:z_2} {c7}, weather severity {m:z_3}, ignition proximity {m:z_4}, fuel load {m:z_5} {c27}, asset exposure {m:z_6}, resource accessibility {m:z_7}, access and road status {m:z_8}, suppression availability {m:z_9} {c31}, and temporal urgency {m:z_10} {c23}. Together they cover threat, exposure, feasibility, and timing.
