OPEN DECISION SPACE - KAPALI KARAR UZAYININ ACILMASI UZERINE CALISMALAR
=======================================================================

A. KAVRAMSAL CERCEVE: KAPALI UZAY NIYE VE NASIL ACILIR
------------------------------------------------------
1. Colorni, A.; Tsoukias, A. (2018). What Is a Decision Problem? Designing Alternatives. Springer. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-319-90599-0_1
   Mekanizma: Bilinen / kismen bilinen / bilinmeyen karar uzayi ayrimi; oznitelik ayristirma ile alternatif tasarimi.

2. Colorni, A.; Tsoukias, A. (2020). Designing alternatives in decision problems. Journal of Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis. DOI: 10.1002/mcda.1709
   Acik erisim: https://hal.archives-ouvertes.fr/hal-02787638/file/jmcda2019-02.pdf
   Mekanizma: 50 yillik literatur taramasi + alternatif tasarimi icin formal cerceve.

3. Hofstede, G.J. (1995). Open problems, formal problems. Journal of Decision Systems. DOI: 10.1080/12460125.1995.10511645
   Mekanizma: Kapalilik bir modelleme tercihidir; acik yonleri modele sabitleme.

4. Levin, M.Sh. (2014). Towards Decision Support Technology Platform for Modular Systems. arXiv.
   Mekanizma: Alternatif secimi -> kompozit alternatiflerin kombinatoryal sentezi (knapsack, assignment, covering).

5. Ferretti, V.; Pluchinotta, I.; Tsoukias, A. (2018). Studying the generation of alternatives in public policy making processes. European Journal of Operational Research. DOI: 10.1016/j.ejor.2018.07.054

6. Arbel, A.; Tong, R. (1982). On the Generation of Alternatives in Decision Analysis Problems. Journal of the Operational Research Society. DOI: 10.1057/JORS.1982.79

7. Del Missier, F.; Visentini, M.; Mantyla, T. (2015). Option generation in decision making: ideation beyond memory retrieval. Frontiers in Psychology. DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2014.01584


B. AKTIF DSS: SISTEMIN KENDISI ALTERNATIF URETIR
------------------------------------------------
8. Fazlollahi, B.; Vahidov, R. (2001). A Method for Generation of Alternatives by Decision Support Systems. Journal of Management Information Systems. DOI: 10.1080/07421222.2001.11045683
   Mekanizma: GA + bulanik kumeler ile parametre uzayinda arama; uretim ve degerlendirmenin ayrilmasi.

9. Brill, E.D.; Flach, J.M.; Hopkins, L.D.; Ranjithan, S. (1990). MGA: a decision support system for complex, incompletely defined problems. IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man and Cybernetics. DOI: 10.1109/21.105076
   Mekanizma: Modeling-to-Generate-Alternatives; yapisal olarak farkli cozumler uretme.

10. Ozernoy, V.M. (1985). Generating Alternatives in Multiple Criteria Decision Making Problems: A Survey. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-642-46536-9_23
    Mekanizma: Morfolojik analiz, parametre-uzayi aramasi.

11. MacCrimmon, K.R.; Wagner, C. (1991). The Architecture of an Information System for the Support of Alternative Generation (GENI). Journal of Management Information Systems. DOI: 10.1080/07421222.1991.11517929

12. Caserio, C. (2012). Knowledge Generating Decision Support Systems: Managing the Trade-Off Between Generating Knowledge and Supporting Decisions. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-7908-2789-7_34
    Not: "open DSS / closed DSS" terimlerini kullanir.

OZET: ACILMA MEKANIZMALARI
--------------------------
1. Oznitelik ayristirma + kombinasyon        -> Colorni & Tsoukias; morfolojik analiz (1, 2, 10)
2. Parametre uzayinda arama / MGA            -> Fazlollahi & Vahidov; Brill et al. (8, 9)
3. Moduler-kombinatoryal sentez              -> Levin (4)
4. Ilkel eylemlerden plan sentezi            -> Chrpa & Vallati; Struss; Yeh & Chang (13, 15, 17)
5. Calisma zamaninda eylem kesfi             -> COWP; Gizzi; Chandak (25, 26, 27)
6. Carpanli eylem uzayi / kod kompozisyonu   -> Tang et al.; CodeAct (32, 40)

TERIM ESLEMESI
--------------
Kapali karar uzayi  : fixed alternative set, catalogue of interventions, known decision space, formal problem
Acik karar uzayi    : alternative generation/design, unknown decision space, composite alternatives,
                      combinatorial/factored action space, open-world planning, unified action space
Aktuator birlestirme: plan synthesis, action composition, coalition formation, capability matching,
                      morphological analysis
